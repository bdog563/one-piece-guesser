    How the clues work

      Hints unlock after 3, 5 and 7 wrong guesses. Tick any mix of character groups for the secret: Easy (the best-known characters), Medium (well-known ones) and Hard (everyone else with enough data). You can always guess any character, whatever is ticked. You won't see the same secret twice until you've been through the whole pool, unless you tick "Allow repeats" (then any character can come up again, just never twice in a row). You can also search by the common English spelling, e.g. "Kaido" or "Kozuki Oden".
